# Competitive landscape and SellFuse position

Reviewed 2026-09-07 from public first-party product pages, documentation, and official app-store listings. Feature claims describe what each vendor publishes; they are not independent verification, and marketplace/API availability can change.

## Current products

| Product            | Publicly positioned strength                                                                                                      | Product implication for SellFuse                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Crosslist          | Photo-generated listings, AI pricing, multichannel inventory, analytics, and sold-item delisting across 11+ destinations          | Do not compete on bulk reseller operations. Make evidence provenance, uncertainty, and the occasional seller's guided journey clearer.                                                       |
| Vendoo             | Crosslisting, inventory, bulk actions, analytics, AI listing enhancement, and sale detection/auto-delisting                       | Keep SellFuse lightweight and task-oriented; avoid turning the home experience into an inventory control panel.                                                                              |

## Position

**SellFuse is the AI selling assistant for ordinary people.**

The core promise is: take pictures; understand what the item probably is and what is still unknown; see evidence-backed price guidance or an honest no-evidence state; choose where to sell; review clear destination-specific drafts; publish through supported direct or assisted paths; mark the item sold once.

Product rules derived from the scan:

- Never present an AI-generated number as a sold comparable.
- Show evidence type, source, observation time, condition, and URL when available.
- Prefer one guided item journey over bulk inventory or professional-reseller dashboards.
- Preserve manual entry at every intelligence failure point.
- Require seller review before any publishing action.
- Describe assisted handoffs accurately when an authorized publishing API is unavailable.
